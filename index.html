About

Treasure Client started with one idea: a 1.12.2 Eaglercraft client that has everything in one place. Your mods, HUD, themes and settings all live in one menu, and the game runs well even on a school Chromebook.

  • 1.12.2Eaglercraft
  • 20+performance mods
  • 5themes
  • Freeand open source
⚡

Fast on weak devices

A built-in performance modpack with a Sodium-style chunk renderer, entity and particle culling, and smoother chunk loading. Good for low-end laptops.

⚔

Made for PvP

1.7 animations, Toggle Sprint, a custom crosshair, hit color, and fast keep-alive replies so your ping stays low.

🌍

Works on newer servers

Modern items and blocks like spears, maces and wind charges show up properly on newer servers that support them.
